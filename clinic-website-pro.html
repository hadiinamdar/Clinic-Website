About

About the doctor

Dr. M. M. Hussain
Founder, Medical Director and Consultant Radiologist & Fetal Medicine Specialist at Kare Scan Center, Austin Town, Bengaluru, Karnataka
Established in 2004

Dr. M. M. Hussain is an experienced Consultant Radiologist with nearly 25 years of experience in the field of diagnostic radiology and medical imaging. He has been providing diagnostic imaging services since 2004 through Kare Scan Center Austin Town, Bengaluru, with a commitment to professional excellence, diagnostic accuracy, and patient care.

With a strong academic background and extensive clinical experience across reputed hospitals and diagnostic centres, Dr. Hussain has developed expertise in diagnostic imaging and has pursued additional training in fetal medicine to further strengthen his professional knowledge.

His approach emphasizes accurate interpretation of medical imaging, clinical correlation, and dependable diagnostic support to referring clinicians and patients.

Practising at Care Scan & Diagnostic Centre lets patients move from consultation to ultrasound, ECG or blood tests without leaving the building, and return with reports for a same-visit review.
